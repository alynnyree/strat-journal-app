Privacy Policy

Strat Journal Auto-Capture (Chrome extension) · last updated 13 September 2026

This extension is a personal tool built for one trader to keep a record of their own trades. It is not a commercial product and it has no users other than the person who installs it.

What it is for

Its single purpose is to capture pictures and screen recordings of the installer's own TradingView chart at the moments their own trade opens, passes fifteen minutes, and closes, and send them to a server the installer runs, so those images can be attached to that trade in their own trading journal.

What it collects

  • Images of the TradingView tab. Still pictures at the three moments above, and — only while the installer has explicitly pressed “Start recording my charts” — a screen recording of that tab covering each trade.
  • The times those images were captured, so they can be matched to the right trade.

It captures only pages on tradingview.com. It cannot capture any other website: the extension does not hold permission for any other site, and it checks the page itself before capturing and refuses otherwise.

What it does not collect

  • No browsing history, no page contents, no form data, no cookies.
  • No names, email addresses, passwords, payment details or financial account credentials.
  • No location, no health data, no personal communications.
  • Nothing at all from any website other than TradingView.

Where it goes

Images are sent directly to a server operated by the installer, at an address the installer types into the extension's own settings. Nothing is sent anywhere else. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party service of any kind receives this data. The developer of this extension does not receive, store or have access to any of it.

How long it is kept

An image waiting to be matched to a trade is deleted from that server once it has been collected, and in any case automatically after 30 days. Images attached to a trade are kept in the installer's own journal until the installer deletes them.

Stored settings

The extension stores two things in Chrome's own local storage on the installer's computer: the address of their server, and the key used to authenticate to it. These never leave that computer except to reach that server.

Selling or sharing

None of this data is sold, transferred, or shared with anyone. It is not used for any purpose unrelated to the single purpose stated above, and it is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Contact

Questions about this extension can be sent to the address listed on its Chrome Web Store listing.
