Trade Info

Strat Setup

2-1-2 Continuation
Inside bar (1) after consolidation, then two directional bars (2) breaking the same side — a directional decision continuing that way.
2-1-2 Reversal
Inside bar (1) after consolidation, then a directional bar (2) breaks one side, followed by a directional bar (2) breaking the opposite side.
3-1-2 Reversal
Played the same as a 2-1-2, but starting from an outside bar (3) instead of a directional bar.
2-2 Continuation
Two directional bars (2) back to back in the same direction, no inside bar between them.
2-2 Reversal
A directional bar (2) in one direction, immediately followed by a directional bar (2) in the opposite direction.
3-2-2 Reversal
Played the same as a 2-2 Reversal, but preceded by an outside bar (3). Stop at 50% of the trigger candle.
1-2-2 Rev Strat
Played the same as a 2-2 Reversal, but starting from an inside bar (1) — has an extra target. Stop at 50% of the trigger candle.
1 Bar Rev Strat
One candle retraces to the 50% level of the previous candle's range, then reverses to take out the opposite side (the "50% Rule").
PMG (Pivot Machine Gun)
A reversal after 5 or more consecutive lower highs or higher lows.

The Play

How you chose this trade — separate from the combo above, which is what you saw. All three aim for at least twice the risk in reward.
Broadening Formation Scalp
Broadening formation on a higher timeframe. Drop to 1 or 5 minute to scalp, entering one edge and targeting the other side of the formation.
FTFC Direction Play
Strat setup taken in the direction the timeframes already agree on. First target is completion of the setup, second is a gap or major pivot. Once a pivot or its liquidity is taken out, looking to reverse.
2s Turning Into 3s
A directional bar (2) expanding into an outside bar (3) — one side taken out, then the other.

Notes

Backtest

Pick a setup, a stretch of time and one or more timeframes. Every occurrence is found in real Schwab candles and played forward using your own stop rule, so you can see what it actually did — then the AI reads those results back to you.
Schwab only keeps about 35 days of minute-by-minute history, so anything longer than that can only be tested on daily bars.

AI Analyst

Reviews every logged trade for setup performance, late/early entries, sub-2:1 R:R setups you took anyway, and exits before target.

Test Classification

Upload a made-up chart — drawn, AI-generated, pulled from the internet — and the AI classifies it strictly from what it sees, with no other hints (no direction, no FTFC, no Broadening Formation). Nothing here is saved as a real trade. Tell it whether it got the pattern right so you build up a record to review later.
+ Add picture or clip
A screen recording works as well as a still — keep clips under about 20 seconds (10MB) so they send from your phone.
Optional — for a real moment in the market. Fill these in and the timeframe alignment is worked out from actual price data instead of guessed from the picture. The picture is still read for everything data cannot show: your drawn lines, a broadening formation, where price sits.
