Strat Setup

2-1-2 Continuation
Inside bar (1) after consolidation, then two directional bars (2) breaking the same side — a directional decision continuing that way.
2-1-2 Reversal
Inside bar (1) after consolidation, then a directional bar (2) breaks one side, followed by a directional bar (2) breaking the opposite side.
3-1-2 Reversal
Played the same as a 2-1-2, but starting from an outside bar (3) instead of a directional bar.
2-2 Continuation
Two directional bars (2) back to back in the same direction, no inside bar between them.
2-2 Reversal
A directional bar (2) in one direction, immediately followed by a directional bar (2) in the opposite direction.
3-2-2 Reversal
Played the same as a 2-2 Reversal, but preceded by an outside bar (3). Stop at 50% of the trigger candle.
1-2-2 Rev Strat
Played the same as a 2-2 Reversal, but starting from an inside bar (1) — has an extra target. Stop at 50% of the trigger candle.
1 Bar Rev Strat
One candle retraces to the 50% level of the previous candle's range, then reverses to take out the opposite side (the "50% Rule").
PMG (Pivot Machine Gun)
A reversal after 5 or more consecutive lower highs or higher lows.
