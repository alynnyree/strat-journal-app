Step B is done. Capture works.

Tap Capture at the bottom, tap the microphone on your keyboard, speak, and it saves to your database with the time on it. The same place your Telegram thoughts go.

CThoughtsEverything you have said, and searching it.

        Marks you can use
        Start with ! to mark it private, and put # in front of words at the very start to tag it, like #biz #pos. Both stay in the text. The same marks the bot understands.
