Step B

Speak a thought

A big empty box lands here. Tap it, tap the microphone on your keyboard, speak, and it saves to your database with the time on it.

Until then, Telegram still catches everything. Nothing is lost by waiting.

Step C

Everything you have said

Newest first, with its tags, and a box to search them. Your thoughts are already being saved, so this screen will have something in it the day it arrives.

Step D

Your goal and where you are

Your ultimate goal at the top, your projects under it, and the roadmap with the step you are on marked. The thing a chat thread can never show you.

Waiting on you Your goals, projects and roadmap tables are empty. Send Claude your seven roadmap steps and which one you are on, and this screen has something to show.

Your password

The one starting brief-, the same one your schedule uses. It is kept on this phone only and never leaves it except to talk to your own database.

Not used yet Nothing on this phone talks to your database until step B. Saving it now means one less thing to do then.

Install it properly

If you opened this in Safari rather than from your home screen, tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens without Safari's bars around it and behaves like an app.
