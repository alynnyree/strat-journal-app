Goal Execution App

Framework Options

Four ways this app could reach you. What each is good at, what it is bad at, and what it costs.

17 Sep 2026 Your database does not change

Before anything else

Changing the framework throws away almost nothing.

Two days of work went into the database, the schema, the message building, the timing, the locks and 93 checks. All of it survives every option below. What changes is only the last few inches: how a thought gets in, and how something reaches you.

What carries over regardless

KeptNine tables, your rules, your checklist, your chat id
KeptThe morning brief logic and its 8:10 timing
KeptReading a message into tags and private
KeptSupabase, the schedule, the locks, every test
ChangesHow you put a thought in
ChangesHow a reminder reaches you
ChangesWhether you can SEE your goals on a screen

The three things driving this

Judge every option against these, not against how modern it sounds.

1Capture without thinkingOne gesture, speak, done. No deciding what it is about first.
2Notifications you actually getA reminder that does not arrive is worse than no reminder.
3Somewhere to SEE itYour goal, your projects, where you are on the roadmap, today's list. A chat thread is the wrong shape for this and always will be.

The four options

A

Stay with Telegram

Built already
Capture
Good. Double tap, keyboard mic, speak, send. Works today.
Notifications
Adequate. Telegram's own. They arrive among your other chats rather than standing out, and a muted chat or a Focus mode silences them without telling you.
Seeing things
Poor, permanently. A roadmap and a task list in a chat thread scroll away. This does not improve with effort.
Reminders
Possible, but they arrive as chat messages.
Costs you
Nothing. It exists.
B

A web app on your home screen, plus Shortcuts

Recommended
Capture
Best of the four. Double tap the back of your phone, speak, done. It goes straight to your database. No app opens, nothing to tap after.
Notifications
Good. Its own alerts, with its own name and icon, separate from your chats. Pushcut is a fallback if Apple's web alerts prove unreliable, and you already use it.
Seeing things
Proper screens. Your goal at the top, your roadmap with the current step marked, today's list, your thoughts. The thing a chat can never be.
Reminders
Its own, or written into Apple Reminders so they behave like any other reminder.
Costs you
Installing it once, the way you installed your trading journal. Nothing on the Mac, no fee, no store.
Why this one
You already run one of these every day. Your trading journal is exactly this, on this phone, and it works. Nothing here is new ground for either of us.
C

Let Apple Reminders be the front

Partial
Capture
Excellent, and free. "Hey Siri, remind me to call the accountant at three." Already works, nothing to build.
Notifications
The best there is. Apple's own, on every device, impossible to miss.
Seeing things
Wrong shape. Reminders has lists. It has no notion of an ultimate goal, a roadmap, or how far along you are.
Reminders
Perfect, obviously.
Costs you
Very little. But on its own it is a to-do app, which your brief says outright this is not.
Best use
Take the good half. Let the app write its time-based reminders into Apple Reminders, and keep the goals, roadmap and thoughts in option B. That combination is genuinely better than either alone.
D

A real iPhone app

Not now
Capture
Best possible. Siri, widgets, the lot.
Notifications
Best possible.
Seeing things
Best possible.
Costs you
A great deal. Xcode on the Mac, an Apple developer account, and on the free tier it stops working roughly every seven days until you plug in and re-sign it. That is 99 dollars a year to avoid.
The real problem
I cannot test it. Everything else here I run and check before you see it. An iPhone app I would be writing blind and you would be the one finding the faults. After the last three days, neither of us wants that.

What I would do

Option B, with C's reminders borrowed. Keep Telegram for now and drop it when you stop reaching for it.
✓Nothing built so far is wastedThe database, the brief, the timing, the tests. All of it is behind whichever front you choose.
✓You have run this exact shape for monthsYour trading journal is a web app on your home screen. Same pattern, same phone, already proven.
✓Capture gets better, not worseDouble tap, speak, done. No app opening, no send button, no chat.
✓Telegram can stay as long as it is usefulTwo ways in is fine. They write to the same place. Drop it when you notice you have stopped using it.
Private project. No AI request leaves this app today, and a test fails if one is ever added.
