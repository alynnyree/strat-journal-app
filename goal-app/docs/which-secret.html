Which Secret Is Which

Four values. Two of them looked identical, which is why they got mixed up. Now they cannot.

Updated 15 Sep 2026 No values on this page Keep yours in Notes

The fix

Every secret now says its own name.

Two of these were 64 characters of random letters and numbers, generated the same way, stored on the same page. Nothing about either one said what it was for. They now start with a word. A glance tells you which you are holding, in Notes, in Supabase, or half way through a command.

The four values

What each one is, how to recognise it, where it lives, and what breaks if you use the wrong one.

brief-...

brief-4f8a91c27b3e6d05af12cc9e74b8301d6e2f5a8b9c0d1e2f3a4b5c6d7e8f9a0b
Recognise it: starts with the word brief.
What it is
The password that lets the morning brief be set off.
Lives in
Supabase Secrets, named BRIEF_TRIGGER_SECRET.
You use it in
The Terminal command that sends the brief, and in the schedule's x-trigger-key header.
Wrong one gives
"The x-trigger-key header was missing or did not match."

hook-...

hook-9c0d1e2f3a4b5c6d7e8f9a0b4f8a91c27b3e6d05af12cc9e74b8301d6e2f5a8b
Recognise it: starts with the word hook.
What it is
The password Telegram hands back so your app knows a message really came from Telegram.
Lives in
Supabase Secrets, named TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET, and Telegram's own records.
You use it in
The one command that points Telegram at your app. Once. Never again.
Wrong one gives
Silence. You message the bot and nothing comes back.

The bot token

8123456789:AAHk3xPq_R2vN9wLmZ4tYbC1dEfGhIjKlMn
Recognise it: it has a colon in the middle. Nothing else of yours does.
What it is
Proof that you own the bot. Telegram gave it to you, not us.
Lives in
Supabase Secrets, named TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, and BotFather.
You use it in
The web address of any command sent to Telegram, straight after the word bot with no space.
Wrong one gives
404 Not Found, from Telegram.
Lost it?
BotFather, /mybots, your bot, API Token. It can always be seen again.

Your chat id

6913699052
Recognise it: about ten digits and nothing else. No letters.
What it is
Which Telegram conversation is yours.
Lives in
Your database, in app_settings. Not a secret, and not in Supabase Secrets.
You use it in
Nothing by hand. The app reads it for itself.

Telling them apart at a glance

The whole point of the change.

1Has a colon?It is the bot token. The only one with a colon.
2Starts with "brief"?It sets off the morning brief.
3Starts with "hook"?It is for Telegram pointing at your app.
4Just digits?It is your chat id, and you never type it anywhere.

Your Notes file

Make one note called Goal App. Paste this in and fill in the blanks once. After that it is copy and paste forever, and there is never anything to replace.

Keep this on your own device It will hold real passwords. Not shared, not in a work account, not pasted into a chat. If one ever does end up somewhere it should not, say so and it gets replaced. That costs three minutes and is never a disaster.
